The phone call

Call Continental Quality Heat Treat first

  • Late most often: 42% of 134 due lines arrived on time. The other five vendors range from 69% to 99%.
  • Late by the most: 61 of the 63 lines that arrived more than 7 days late across all vendors are Continental's ($660k of $666k received value). When late, the median delay is 17 days (worst 28). Every other vendor lands 99% or more of its lines within 7 days.
  • Warns it will be late, then misses its own date too: 91% of its confirmations promise a date after our required date (avg +8.5 days; other vendors avg +0.2 to +2.6). It then meets only 58% of its own promises.
  • Not getting better: 45% on time for lines due Sep–Dec (n=56) vs 40% for Jan–May (n=78). It has been consistently poor, not a recent slip.
  • Material: $1.51M received (5.4% of spend) in outside heat-treat services. 7 open lines are past due today ($14k).

What to ask them

  • Our planned lead time for heat treat is 19 days from PO; you quote 28 and deliver in 24.5 (medians). Can you commit to a standard lead time we can load into MRP?
  • When you do confirm a date, you meet it 58% of the time. What is driving the misses: furnace capacity, batching, or incoming parts?
  • Split shipments: 51% of lines come in more than one shipment. Can partial lots be avoided or flagged?

What to fix on our side

Beacon side: our 19-day heat-treat lead time is about 9 days shorter than what Continental quotes. Part of this lateness is ours to fix in planning.

Vendor part numbers: the cleanup, started

Two vendors confirm with their own part numbers (241 confirmation rows have a vendor PN and no Beacon part). Joining each back to its PO line recovers a crosswalk. The history exposes three problems a cleanup has to design for:

  • The same vendor PN means different parts at different vendors. K-1050 is a cold-headed pin blank at Heritage and a heading punch at Ostmark, so the key must be vendor + vendor PN.
  • Vendors renumber. This week's PDFs use APH-441-OS and OST-CAR-A-100 for parts the ERP knows as APH-4411 and WZ-HM-EIN. A mapping needs first-seen/last-seen dates and a review status, not just a lookup.
  • Look-alikes are real. APH-441 and APH-4411 are two different blanks (9472-3 vs 9472-4), so fuzzy matching on PNs would merge them. Mappings are proposed from qty and price evidence and approved by a person.

How the numbers were built

The ERP extract has traps that change the answer if taken at face value:

+$76kif Ostmark prices were converted from EUR again
Data problems found and how each was handled
Keyed-in-error receipts reversed (RV + the R it cancels)
50 · Dropped both rows for shipment dates/counts; nets to zero for value.
Unreversed duplicate receipts (same line+qty within 7 days, pushes line over ordered)
1 · Excluded: txn 701546
receipt_txn.txn_date format
MM/DD/YYYY · Parsed explicitly; SQLite date() returns NULL on all rows.
po_line.qty_received disagrees with receipt_txn
169 · Field ignored; receipts are the source of truth.
Superseded confirmations
30 · Only the live (latest) confirmation is used.
Confirmation feed ends (last doc_date)
2026-04-03 · Confirmation metrics use POs dated on/before 2026-03-30 only.
Cancelled POs
5 · Excluded everywhere (none have receipts).
Ostmark EUR confirmations / PO price ratio
0.92 · PO prices are USD already; no FX applied to received value.
qc_hold dates before the PO existed
16 · Hold dates unreliable -> holds shown as counts only, not ranked on.
Unused/decoy tables
gl_account, ship_method, user_master, plant_calendar, mrp_message · No link to vendor performance (MRP messages reflect Beacon demand changes).
Metric definitions
Received value
Sum of net receipt qty (R minus reversals; unreversed duplicate excluded) x PO line unit price, bucketed by receipt month. USD. Receipts carry no price, so this is value at PO price, not invoiced cost.
Due lines
Non-cancelled PO lines with required date on/before the as-of date and at least one valid receipt.
On-time %
Due lines whose LAST valid receipt (the one that completes the line) is on/before the required date / due lines.
Severely late
Due lines whose last receipt is more than 7 days after the required date.
$ severely late
Received value (capped at ordered qty) on severely late lines.
Promised late %
Live confirmations whose promised date is after our required date / lines with a live confirmation.
On-time vs promise
Lines received on/before the vendor's own promised date / lines with a live confirmation.
Lead time (median days from PO date)
Allowed = required - PO date; Quoted = promised - PO date; Actual = last receipt - PO date.
Past-due open
OPEN lines with required date before as-of and received qty < ordered; value = balance x unit price.
Price above PO
Live USD confirmations priced above the PO line; exposure = price difference x ordered qty.
Trend
On-time % for lines required Sep-Dec 2025 vs Jan-May 2026; Fisher exact test p-value (p<0.05 = real change).
What this readout cannot tell you
  • Whether any late delivery stopped production or caused a customer expedite: there is no production, downtime or customer-order data in the extract.
  • Invoiced cost or price variance paid: receipts carry no price and there is no invoice/AP table. Value is at PO price.
  • Quality performance: qc_hold has only 35 holds and 16 are dated before their PO existed, so holds are shown as counts, not ranked.
  • Confirmation behaviour after March: the confirmation feed stops at 04/03/2026, so April–May POs have none in the ERP.
  • Why required dates are set where they are: if the 19-day heat-treat lead time is a planning default, part of Continental's "lateness" is Beacon's own.
